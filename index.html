Dear Akka,

Happy Birthday to my favourite human!💗

Honestly, I don't even know where to start. Manam entha godava padathamo, entha arusthamo, entha okarini okaram irritate chesthamo manake telusu. 😂 Sometimes I wonder how we even tolerate each other! But at the end of the day, no matter how much we fight, there is nobody in this world with whom I share a bond as pure and beautiful as ours.

Nuvvu naaku just sister kaadhu ra, you're literally my energy drink! 😭❤️ Naaku bore kottina, mood off aina, or just ala khaali ga unna, I'll come and disturb you for absolutely no reason. And neeku bore kottina, nuvvu naa daggariki vachi nannu disturb chesthav. Mana pani adhe ga! 😂 We irritate each other, fight over the smallest things, scream at each other, and five minutes later we're back to being our usual stupid selves. And honestly, I wouldn't trade this madness for anything.

From childhood, there have been so many moments when I genuinely felt like, "I can't get through this day without my sister." Sometimes I don't even need to tell you anything. Just having you around makes things feel better. Nuvvu pakkana unte chaalu, somehow everything feels a little less boring and a little more okay. You have no idea how many times you've made my mood better without even realising it. 🥹

And you know what I love the most? Mana iddaram amma tho kalisi unde moments. Those are genuinely my favourite memories. The three of us laughing, talking, fighting over silly things, and just being together. I wish we could freeze those moments sometimes. Amma manalni iddarini choosthu, mana iddariki manchi jaragali, manam life lo baaga settle avvali ani korukuntundi. And I really hope we make her proud and give her all the happiness she deserves. ❤️

You're growing up so fast, and sometimes I look at you and wonder how time is passing this quickly. But for me, you'll always be that small, cute little girl who's somehow my elder sister. 😂❤️ Peddha akka aina kuda, naaku nuvvu eppatiki naa chinna pillave. I love you more than all our fights, all our silly arguments, and all the times you've annoyed me put together. 🥹💗 Happy Birthday, my little big sister. Nuvvu eppudu happy ga undali, nee dreams anni nijam avvali, and mana bond eppatiki ilage undali.

Love you endlessly, Akka. 🫂💗
